## 2. Common Everyday Verbs — 30 words

| # | Spanish | Pronunciation | English | Type | Key Forms | Example (ES → EN) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ser | ser | to be (identity, traits, origin) | verb (irreg.) | yo soy, tú eres | Mi madre es médica. → My mother is a doctor. |
| 2 | estar | es-TAR | to be (location, temporary states) | verb (irreg.) | yo estoy, tú estás | Mi padre está en casa. → My father is at home. |
| 3 | tener | te-NER | to have | verb (irreg.) | yo tengo, tú tienes (e→ie) · tener ___ años = to be ___ years old | Tengo tres hermanos. → I have three siblings. |
| 4 | hacer | a-SER | to do, to make | verb (irreg.) | yo hago, tú haces | ¿Qué haces? → What are you doing? |
| 5 | ir | eer | to go | verb (irreg.) | yo voy, tú vas · ir a + verb = going to | Voy a la casa de mi abuela. → I'm going to my grandmother's house. |
| 6 | poder | po-DER | can, to be able to | verb (stem-change) | yo puedo, tú puedes (o→ue) | ¿Puedes venir hoy? → Can you come today? |
| 7 | querer | ke-RER | to want; to love (people) | verb (stem-change) | yo quiero, tú quieres (e→ie) | Mi hijo quiere un perro. → My son wants a dog. |
| 8 | haber | a-BER | there is / there are (as hay) | verb (irreg.) | present: hay (same for singular and plural) | Hay cinco personas en mi familia. → There are five people in my family. |
| 9 | venir | be-NEER | to come | verb (irreg.) | yo vengo, tú vienes (e→ie) | Mi tía viene mañana. → My aunt is coming tomorrow. |
| 10 | ver | ber | to see; to watch | verb (irreg.) | yo veo, tú ves | Veo a mis primos los domingos. → I see my cousins on Sundays. |
| 11 | saber | sa-BER | to know (facts); to know how to | verb (irreg.) | yo sé, tú sabes | No sé. → I don't know. |
| 12 | conocer | ko-no-SER | to know (people, places); to meet | verb (irreg.) | yo conozco, tú conoces | Conozco a tu hermano. → I know your brother. |
| 13 | dar | dar | to give | verb (irreg.) | yo doy, tú das | Le doy un regalo a mi madre. → I give my mother a present. |
| 14 | tomar | to-MAR | to take; to drink | verb | yo tomo, tú tomas | Mi abuelo toma café. → My grandfather drinks coffee. |
| 15 | poner | po-NER | to put, to place | verb (irreg.) | yo pongo, tú pones | Pongo el libro en la mesa. → I put the book on the table. |
| 16 | necesitar | ne-se-see-TAR | to need | verb | yo necesito, tú necesitas | Necesito ayuda. → I need help. |
| 17 | deber | de-BER | should, must; to owe | verb | yo debo, tú debes · deber + verb = should | Debes llamar a tu madre. → You should call your mother. |
| 18 | llevar | ye-BAR | to carry, to take (somewhere); to wear | verb | yo llevo, tú llevas | Llevo a los niños a la escuela. → I take the children to school. |
| 19 | pasar | pa-SAR | to happen; to pass; to spend (time) | verb | yo paso, tú pasas | ¿Qué pasa? → What's happening? / What's up? |
| 20 | buscar | boos-KAR | to look for | verb | yo busco, tú buscas | Busco a mi hermana. → I'm looking for my sister. |
| 21 | encontrar | en-kon-TRAR | to find | verb (stem-change) | yo encuentro, tú encuentras (o→ue) | No encuentro mis llaves. → I can't find my keys. |
| 22 | dejar | de-HAR | to leave (something); to let | verb | yo dejo, tú dejas | Dejo el coche aquí. → I leave the car here. |
| 23 | usar | oo-SAR | to use | verb | yo uso, tú usas | Mi abuela no usa el teléfono. → My grandmother doesn't use the phone. |
| 24 | abrir | a-BREER | to open | verb | yo abro, tú abres | Mi padre abre la puerta. → My father opens the door. |
| 25 | cerrar | se-RRAR | to close | verb (stem-change) | yo cierro, tú cierras (e→ie) | ¿Puedes cerrar la ventana? → Can you close the window? |
| 26 | empezar | em-pe-SAR | to start, to begin | verb (stem-change) | yo empiezo, tú empiezas (e→ie) | La clase empieza a las nueve. → The class starts at nine. |
| 27 | terminar | ter-mee-NAR | to finish, to end | verb | yo termino, tú terminas | Termino a las cinco. → I finish at five. |
| 28 | ayudar | a-yoo-DAR | to help | verb | yo ayudo, tú ayudas | Ayudo a mi madre. → I help my mother. |
| 29 | esperar | es-pe-RAR | to wait (for); to hope | verb | yo espero, tú esperas | Espero a mi novio. → I'm waiting for my boyfriend. |
| 30 | traer | tra-ER | to bring | verb (irreg.) | yo traigo, tú traes | ¿Qué traes? → What are you bringing? |

*Pronunciation note: Spanish **v** sounds like **b**, so venir = be-NEER. In Spain, **z** and **c** (before e/i) sound like "th": hacer = a-THER.*

### 🔗 Combine It

- Mi hermano tiene un hijo y quiere otro. → My brother has a son and wants another one.
- Hoy voy a ver a mis abuelos. → Today I'm going to see my grandparents.
- ¿Puedes ayudar a tu prima? → Can you help your cousin?
