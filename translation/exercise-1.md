# Translation Exercise
  
**Your Task**: Translate the following story into Spanish.

## English Passage:
- Today is a special day:my grandmother is eighty years old. In the morning, I help my mother in the kitchen. My father can't find the car keys, but my brother knows where they are. At eleven, we go to my grandmother's house. My grandfather is waiting at the door. There are a lot of people in the house. My uncle comes from Madrid, and he brings a big present. My cousin Laura is here with her new boyfriend. I don't know her boyfriend, but he is very nice. My grandmother opens the present and says that she is very happy. We are going to eat at two, and after lunch, my cousins want to take my grandmother to the park.

## Spanish Passage translated by me:
- Hoy es un día especial: mi abuela cumple ochenta años. Por la mañana, ayudo a mi madre en la cocina. Mi padre no encuentra las llaves del coche, pero mi hermano sabe dónde están. A las once, vamos a casa de mi abuela. Mi abuelo está esperando en la puerta. Hay mucha gente en la casa. Mi tío viene de Madrid y trae un regalo grande. Mi prima Laura está aquí con su nuevo novio. No conozco a su novio, pero es muy simpático. Mi abuela abre el regalo y dice que está muy contenta. Vamos a comer a las dos y, después de comer, mis primos quieren llevar a mi abuela al parque.
